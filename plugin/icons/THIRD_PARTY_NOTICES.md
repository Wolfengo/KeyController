# KeyController icon

The fingerprint in `mark.svg` is adapted from Google Material Icons, licensed under Apache-2.0. See [LICENSE.material-icons](LICENSE.material-icons).

Original: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/d3d4aca5a7cf50bc68bbd401cefa708e364194e8/src/action/fingerprint/materialicons/24px.svg

Modifications: scaled, adjusted to a heavier line weight, tinted using `currentColor`, and given a transparent cutout around a horizontal key. The shield, SSH banner and key are KeyController artwork. SSH lettering is vector geometry with transparent cutouts, independent of installed fonts.

The compact `bar.svg` shield and key are KeyController artwork and contain no Material Icons fingerprint geometry.
